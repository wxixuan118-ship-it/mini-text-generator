Cat Symbol FAQ

Why are there so many cat face emoji?

Blame Japan — or thank it, depending on how you feel about cats. Cat faces were wildly popular in early Japanese mobile emoji sets (especially on DoCoMo phones) long before Unicode standardised them. When Unicode adopted emoji in 2010, the nine cat expressions came along as a full set because they were already so beloved.

What is the difference between 😺 grinning cat and 🐱 cat face?

😺 is a cat version of the grinning smiley 😀 — mouth open, cheerful, expressive. 🐱 is a plain, neutral cat face with a closed mouth, more like a stylised cat icon than a specific emotion. Think of 😺 as the chatty, friendly cat and 🐱 as the quiet, observant one sitting on the windowsill.

Do cat emoji have different personalities?

Absolutely, and people treat them like distinct characters. 😺 is upbeat and friendly, 😸 is shy but happy, 😹 laughs at everything, 😻 is the affectionate one who loves everyone, 😼 has mischief planned, 😽 is sweet and kissy, 🙀 is dramatic and easily shocked, 😿 mopes dramatically, and 😾 is permanently grumpy. Each has its own fan club.

Which cat symbol should I use for a cozy vibe?

For cozy and warm, go with 🐱 the plain cat face — it is calm, neutral and feels like a cat curled up on a blanket. For dreamy cozy, pair it with 🌙 or ✨. For playful cozy, 😺 the grinning cat adds a little energy without going over the top. And the paw prints 🐾 make perfect cozy dividers.

Why is the black cat 🐈‍⬛ a ZWJ sequence?

The black cat is what Unicode calls a ZWJ (zero-width joiner) sequence. It glues three characters together — the cat 🐈, an invisible joiner character, and a black square ⬛ — to make one combined image. Very old devices that do not understand ZWJ sequences will show the two parts separately: 🐈⬛ instead of 🐈‍⬛.

How many cat-related emoji are there in Unicode?

Counting faces, full-body cats and related animals, there are nine cat-face expressions (😺 through 😾), plus 🐱 cat face, 🐈 cat, 🐈‍⬛ black cat and 🐾 paw prints — twelve cat-specific characters. Add in the big cats (🦁 lion, 🐯 tiger, 🐆 leopard) and the cat-adjacent mammals, and the feline family is surprisingly large.
