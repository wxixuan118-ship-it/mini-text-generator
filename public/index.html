Small text FAQ

How does the small text generator work?

It maps each normal letter to a look-alike character from the Unicode standard — the same character set your device already uses for emoji and symbols. Small caps come from the Phonetic Extensions block, while superscript and subscript letters come from the Superscripts and Subscripts block. Because they are real characters, you can copy and paste them anywhere plain text is accepted.

Why are some letters not converted?

Unicode doesn't include a small version of every letter. Subscript in particular is missing several letters (like d, f, g, q, w, y and z), so those are left in their normal form. Superscript and small caps cover the full alphabet.

Will the tiny text show up correctly on every device?

Almost always. Modern phones, computers and browsers ship with fonts that support these characters. On very old systems a few letters might appear as boxes, but that is increasingly rare.

Is this small text a font?

No. Fonts change how existing letters look, but they don't survive copy-paste. This tool changes the letters themselves into different Unicode characters, so the small style stays wherever you paste it.

Can I use small text in usernames and display names?

Usually, yes. Instagram, TikTok, Discord, Twitch and most games accept Unicode characters in display names and bios, so small caps or tiny text pasted from this tool will show up correctly. A few platforms restrict usernames (not display names) to plain letters and numbers — if a site rejects the text, try it in the display name or bio field instead.

Does it work with numbers, emojis and other languages?

Numbers convert cleanly in superscript and subscript (¹²³ and ₁₂₃). Emojis, punctuation and non-Latin scripts such as Chinese, Japanese, Arabic or Cyrillic have no small Unicode equivalents, so they pass through unchanged. You can still mix them with small text — for example ꜱᴍᴀʟʟ ᴛᴇxᴛ ✨ — and the result copies as a single string.

Yes — it's completely free, works in your browser, and nothing you type is sent to a server.
