Tips for a better typing text GIF

Use a short headline or call to action so viewers can read the complete animation before it loops. High contrast — such as white text on a dark background — improves readability. A 600 × 300 GIF is a good all-purpose choice; smaller dimensions create a lighter file, while 1080 × 540 gives you more resolution for presentations and larger embeds.

Animated GIF does not support modern full-color pixels as efficiently as video, so flat backgrounds and simple text are ideal. If a platform has a strict upload limit, shorten the message, select a smaller canvas, or choose a faster typing speed to reduce the number of frames.
