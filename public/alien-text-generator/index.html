Alien text generator FAQ

Where do the alien letters come from?

They are ordinary Unicode characters borrowed from real writing systems. The main alphabet pulls from the IPA Extensions block plus Armenian, Cyrillic and African letters — ր is an Armenian reh, օ an Armenian oh, Ь a Cyrillic soft sign. The block style uses Canadian Aboriginal syllabics. Nothing here is invented; it just looks unfamiliar to English readers, which is exactly the effect.

Is it disrespectful to use letters from other alphabets?

It is worth knowing what you are using. These are living scripts for millions of people, and this generator is picking characters purely for their shape. For a game tag or a username that is generally taken as harmless, but it is a reason not to use these characters in anything official, and a reason to avoid stringing them into something that imitates real words in those languages.

Will alien text work in my username?

In display names and bios on Instagram, TikTok, Discord, Steam and most games, yes. Some platforms restrict the underlying @handle to plain ASCII and will reject it. A few games also filter unusual scripts specifically to stop impersonation, so test before you build a whole identity around it.

Why do some letters stay normal?

The block alphabet has no good syllabics match for E, G, I, K, O, T and Y, so those are left as plain capitals rather than substituted with something misleading. Mixing them in actually helps legibility — fully converted text gets hard to read fast.

What is the glitch style doing?

It adds two combining marks to every character, which stack above and below the letter and make it look corrupted. It is a restrained version of the “Zalgo” effect — enough to read as broken transmission without the usual wall of overlapping marks that breaks line spacing in chat apps.

Can people search for my alien name?

No. ǟ is not the letter a as far as any search index is concerned, so nobody will find your profile by typing the plain spelling. Keep a searchable handle and use the alien text for the display name.

Is the alien text generator free?

Yes — free, no account, and the conversion happens entirely in your browser.
